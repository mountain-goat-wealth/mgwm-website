Why “Mountain Goat”

Every big climb starts with a good route.

Our name comes from the trail-running group where it all started, the Mountain Goats of Millcreek Canyon. Nimble, steady, and comfortable on steep terrain.

When Bobby isn't meeting with clients, you'll often find him somewhere like Wyoming's Wind River Range, where reaching the summit is only half the goal. Getting back down is the other half.

Retirement works the same way. You study the terrain, prepare for changing weather, and choose a pace you can sustain for the long haul. That's the mindset behind every Retirement Roadmap.

Common Questions

What people ask before they request a roadmap.

Is it really complimentary?

Yes. The roadmap has no fee and no obligation. If you later want a comprehensive plan or ongoing management, we'll explain those costs up front.

Do I need a certain amount saved?

No investment minimums. The roadmap is designed for working professionals 40 and over who are thinking seriously about retirement.

Why is there an agreement?

A written roadmap is personalized advice, so we document it in a short planning agreement. There's no fee.

What if I'm not in Utah, Texas, or Wyoming?

We're currently registered in those three states today. However, if you're outside, reach out anyway since we may still be able to manage your account or register in your state.

Who will I meet with?

Bobby Goodman, CFP®, with support from our advisor team.

I think I am too close to retirement to fix anything.

The five years before retirement are when good planning has the biggest dollar impact: sequence-of-returns risk, Social Security timing, Roth conversions, and tax-efficient withdrawals all happen in that window. We can usually help clients find meaningful opportunities in the runway.

What do I need to bring?

Nothing. Genuinely. No statements, no spreadsheets, no document gathering. Just be ready to talk about where you are and what you want. If we agree to go further, that's when we'd ask for the formal documents.

What does 'fiduciary' actually mean for me?

It means we will put your interests above ours, on every recommendation, every time. We will always disclose how we're compensated and will never recommend a product that doesn't serve your interests first.

How do you charge?

We are fee-based for our wealth management clients — a transparent, percentage-based fee on the assets we manage or may receive a commission on insurance products. We will tell you the exact number on our first meeting, before you ever consider becoming a client. No hidden costs.

I don't think I have enough money to work with you.

We don't have investment minimums. We work with clients across the climb — from professionals laying the foundation to retirees navigating distribution. The only requirement for our first meeting is that you're serious about getting answers.
