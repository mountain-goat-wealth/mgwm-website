Common questions

Is the Retirement Roadmap really free?
Yes. Both meetings and your written roadmap are complimentary, with no obligation to become a client afterward.
Will Bobby try to sell me something?
No. As a fiduciary, Bobby is required to act in your best interest. The roadmap is about clarity, not a pitch — if working together further makes sense, that's a conversation for later, on your terms.
What should I bring to the planning session?
A rough sense of your savings, retirement accounts, and income sources is helpful, but nothing is required ahead of time — Bobby will guide the conversation.
How is this different from a full financial plan?
The Roadmap is a focused starting point — a clear picture of where you stand and your next steps. A full financial plan goes deeper and is something you'd pursue only if and when it makes sense for you.
