Our Team

The Advisors Behind the Climb

We are a remote-first team of financial advisors serving clients in Utah, Texas, and Wyoming. Our goal is to help you conquer your climb to retirement.

Robert "Bobby" Goodman
Founder
Salt Lake City, UT
CFP® MBA · University of Utah B.S. Economics · BYU

Bobby spent 18 years at Goldman Sachs Private Wealth managing complex strategies for ultra-high-net-worth families before founding Mountain Goat Wealth Management in 2025. He left Wall Street with one goal: bring the same institutional discipline to families on Main Street who deserve it just as much. As Founder and Chief Compliance Officer, Bobby sets the strategic direction of the firm and works directly with clients on comprehensive wealth management. His reputation as a natural teacher means clients always understand the why behind every decision — not just the what. Outside the office, Bobby can be found mountain biking the Wasatch trails, fly fishing, or trail running.

Cade Hyde
Financial Advisor
Salt Lake City, UT
B.S. Supply Chain Management · BYU

Cade has spent 5 years as a data analyst, a background that explains the precision and detailed diligence he applies to every client relationship. He excels at translating complex financial anxieties into clear, actionable plans, and clients consistently describe his communication as thoughtful and easy to understand. Cade is passionate about helping younger professionals build the financial foundation that sets up every future decade for success. Outside the office, he is likely trail running, on the golf course, or cycling.

Michael Goodman
Financial Advisor
Houston, TX
B.S. Geology · BYU

Michael has spent years as a consultant managing oilfield remediation budgets and complex risk assessments. That experience guides the calm, methodical approach he now applies to wealth management. What It Means to Have a Fiduciary in Your Corner

We Put You First

A fiduciary is legally obligated to act in your best interest at all times, and not just ethically encouraged to. This is a binding standard, not a marketing claim.

No Hidden Conflicts of Interest

We do not recommend a product because it pays us a higher commission. Every recommendation we make will genuinely serve your financial plan and your goals.

Transparent, Candid Communication

You will always know exactly what we recommend, why we recommend it, and what it costs. No jargon, no fine print, no surprises.
