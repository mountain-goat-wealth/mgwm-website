The Mountain Goat Story

Born on a Trail Run in
Millcreek Canyon

For 18 years, I was good at my job at Goldman Sachs. Really good. But somewhere between the long hours, the frantic pace, and the ultra-high-net-worth clients who expected the world, I realized something was missing. I wasn't helping people in any way that felt meaningful. I had 18 years of experience, and I was spending it on people who needed it least.

On a Saturday morning in the spring of 2024, my friend Cade Hyde and I set out on a 10-mile trail run through Millcreek Canyon. We'd been talking about the idea the whole way — somewhere between Elbow Fork and Rattlesnake Trail, between climbs. Then we hit the Overlook. Salt Lake Valley spread out below us, perfect morning light, the whole valley visible in every direction. That's where it sank in. Mountain Goat Wealth Management was born at 7,000 feet, staring down at the families we wanted to help.

The name came naturally. Our running group had always called themselves the Mountain Goats. Nimble. Agile. Comfortable on steep terrain. It felt right for a firm built to guide people through one of life's most challenging climbs. I brought the Wall Street discipline. Cade brought marketing and analytics. Together we built the firm I had always wished existed.
